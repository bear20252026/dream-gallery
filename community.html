General Principles

To build an open, friendly and rewarding gallery community, and to maintain a good atmosphere for browsing and creation, we have formulated these Guidelines under applicable laws and in combination with the Terms of Service and the Privacy Guidelines. Whenever you upload, publish, spread content or interact in the Gallery, you shall comply with laws, public order and good morals, and with these Guidelines.

We encourage you to upload and share: content that promotes core socialist values and displays fine traditional Chinese culture and the spirit of the times; content recording sincere moments and passing on warmth; content that helps build consensus and answer questions; and other content of taste, class and responsibility.

The following chapters classify prohibited content and disposal rules. Every prohibition is a red line. Violations are determined by these Guidelines; where laws provide otherwise, those provisions prevail.

Part Two · Community Red Lines

The following are red lines no user may cross. Every prohibition guards the consensus above.

1. Illegality & Prohibited Content

It is strictly forbidden to publish the following:

  • Content endangering the socialist system, or attacking or negating the guiding ideology and action guidelines of the Party and the state;
  • Content splitting the country or harming national sovereignty and territorial integrity (including misuse of national symbols, callsigns, titles and maps);
  • Content harming national honour and interests (insulting the national anthem, flag, emblem or currency; defaming revolutionary leaders, heroes and martyrs, or public servants and Party members);
  • Content leaking important Party and state secrets (privately filming military operations, disclosing coordinates of military sites, leaking unpublished documents and deployments);
  • Content harming ethnic and regional unity (mocking or hurting ethnic/regional feelings, rendering confrontation, defaming ethnic customs);
  • Content violating national religious policy (religious extremism, cults and superstition, using religion to undermine the system and unity);
  • Spreading terrorism or extremism (advocating terrorist acts, showing violent terror imagery or symbols);
  • Glorifying wars of aggression and colonial history (advocating fascism, extreme nationalism, using colonial-flavoured wording and imagery);
  • Spreading or marketing feudal superstition (recruiting for profit, sorcery, divination for healing or fortune-changing, conspiracy theories via superstition);
  • Promoting, producing, selling or using prohibited items (guns and ammunition, controlled knives, explosives and dangerous chemicals, drugs and narcotic/psychotropic substances, reactionary publications, etc.);
  • Infringing the lawful rights of individuals or organisations (copyright infringement, impersonating rights holders, leaking privacy and trade secrets, insult, defamation, harming reputation and goodwill);
  • Other illegal or non-compliant content (gambling tricks, organised crime, fake certificates and tickets, illegal trading of personal information, surrogacy, illegal adoption, illegal finance, illegal circulation of tobacco and alcohol, lock-picking tools, drone hacking, false fundraising, domestic violence, illegal equipment, all kinds of fraud, abusing/killing protected animals, illegally introducing or releasing alien species, etc.).

2. Violating Public Order & Good Morals

  • Terror, blood, violence, freakish or disgusting content causing strong discomfort (severe injury/bleeding, blood-horror as a selling point, abusing animals, corpses, suicide/accident scenes, jump scares);
  • Content causing disgust (excrement, filth, trypophobia imagery, freakish eating);
  • Expressing opinions or marketing with aggressive catchphrases, internet slang, profanity or insulting gestures (generic-kin slurs, stigmatising homophones, group-confrontation terms, insulting acts);
  • Content risking serious injury or death (choking/electric-shock challenges, eating toxic substances, dangerous driving disturbing traffic);
  • Instigating, teaching or promoting self-harm or suicide, showing tendencies or expressing suicidal intent;
  • Risk-seeking behaviour for attention or marketing (death-defying challenges, prank stunts, displaying lethal weapons);
  • Spreading bad trends and values (smoking, drinking, flaunting wealth, money worship; AI face-swap risks; pseudo-superstition; mocking disasters; bottomless ugly-idol worship; damaging the environment; fighting violence with violence; benefiting at others' expense; improper views on relations; paid companionship; malicious group marketing; exploiting the deceased for sympathy; "sang" defeatist culture; vulgar wedding pranks; wasting food and binge eating; distorting classics; following disgraced celebrities);
  • Other content not advocated by law and social morality.

3. Pornography & Vulgarity

  • Publishing obscene or pornographic information, or promoting/redirecting to it;
  • Publishing imagery, acts, words, text or sounds with sexual innuendo, teasing, inducement or association.

4. Harming Minors' Physical & Mental Health

  • Content involving minors' sexual acts, nudity, sexual transactions, molestation or harassment; showing school or domestic violence, cyberbullying or doxxing of minors; showing minors' drug use, gambling or illegal child labour;
  • Inducing minors to publish content leaking personal information; improperly showing names, faces, addresses or schools of minors under 14 without guardian consent;
  • Showing minors' smoking, drinking or tattoos, profanity or cosmetic procedures, school-weariness, entering commercial entertainment venues, joining gangs; showing extreme fan-circle behaviour (fan wars, vote-baiting); causing extreme emotions, inducing high-risk imitation, spreading bad slang, promoting ghostwritten homework or bullying, providing companion-play or boosting services, or advocating drug abuse;
  • Spreading content affecting minors' values (corrupt cartoons, wealth-flaunting, early marriage/dating/pregnancy, cracking anti-addiction systems, negative self-deprecation, extravagant decadence, pseudo-science scaremongering, bad views on friendship or the uselessness of study);
  • Exploiting minors for profit (merchandising, inducing tipping, using their image for traffic, controversial personas for clicks, improper tests and judgements, sensationalising minors' crimes);
  • Displaying content that may affect minors' physical and mental health without a prominent notice.

5. Violating Science & Truthfulness

  • Impersonating persons or organisations (usernames, avatars, brands) to pretend to be state organs, news media, social organisations or other accounts;
  • Publishing information proven to be rumour or inconsistent with facts; distorting, tampering with or fabricating misleading information;
  • Over-generalising or falsely assigning causality; using time-expired information to interpret recent events; repeating hearsay, quoting out of context, fabricating or exaggerating;
  • Content violating scientific common sense or misinterpreting scientific principles; unverified historical rumours and distorted history; sensational and unwarranted content; distorting interpretations without reliable sources or fabricating authoritative opinions; material errors in objective facts (names, data, times, places);
  • Using deep learning, virtual reality and other new technologies to produce or spread non-genuine audio/video without prominent labelling;
  • Providing internet news information services beyond licensing; publishing finance, medical, judicial or educational professional content requires corresponding qualifications — publishing financial product recommendations, medication guidance, legal interpretation or paid education/training without qualifications is prohibited.

6. Unfriendly Conduct

  • Attacking others for unfair competition or traffic; insults, personal attacks, vulgar profanity in discussions; harassment, coercion, threats or malicious cursing;
  • Exposing others' private information (real names, phones, addresses, contacts, photos) without permission, or using public information to harass; naming-and-shaming or exposing accounts likely to cause cyberbullying;
  • Negative statements contrary to facts or emotionally inflamed; groundless bias; inciting or instigating radical follow-the-crowd acts; malicious labelling; false-flag smears and deliberate provocation; clipping and relaying controversial fragments to split the community;
  • "Dark jokes" entertainment-ising death, illness, disability or disasters; improper behaviour at solemn commemorations; violence and hatred toward disaster victims and their families;
  • Inciting group confrontation, creating group conflicts, spreading extreme hatred; insulting others' occupations, status or physical features; prejudice and discrimination against any group;
  • Extreme fan-circle behaviour (mutual scolding, fan wars, mindless rants, false exposés, romance hype, calling for rankings/fundraising/account buying/traffic brushing/control-commenting, organised paid trolls, advocating traffic supremacy and deformed aesthetics).

7. Disrupting Platform Order

  • Improperly inducing data growth (prize interactions, incentivised transactions and fake data); using automated systems to brush comments or buying fake data from third parties;
  • Deceptive, misleading, ambiguous or out-of-context covers and headlines for attention;
  • Publishing large amounts of repetitive, purposeless or irrelevant spam; batch auto-generated content; sock-puppet submissions; grey-industry funnel-selling;
  • Counterfeit, shoddy or pirated spam advertising and non-compliant promotion; improperly funneling users to third-party platforms for registration or transactions;
  • Displaying, teaching or promoting game plug-ins and private servers;
  • Repeatedly uploading violating content ignoring rules and notices, or anomalously swapping sources to replace data;
  • Abusing platform functions (malicious reporting, fabricating evidence for reports, exploiting loopholes for profit, improper funneling);
  • Disposing of account usage rights by gift, loan, transfer, sale, pledge, inheritance or licensing to others;
  • Unauthorised use of brand wording such as "in cooperation with the Gallery" or "co-produced with the Gallery" for promotion.

8. Disposal of Violations

For content violating these Guidelines we will, per severity: remind and label, restrict display, fold, delete and take offline, and remove violating information. For violating visitors: clear nicknames, restrict functions, blacklist.

Disposal follows the gradient principle: escalating with accumulated violations and severity — leniency for first offences, strictness for repeat offenders; for major social harm, serious adverse influence or extremely bad circumstances, the gradient does not apply and we go straight to strictness up to permanent banning. Frequent-reported visitors will be warned first and observed. Disposal information may be published as notices; illegal matters are reported to the relevant authorities by law.

For content infringing others' lawful rights, rights holders may complain via the "notice" procedure in the Terms of Service; the disposed party may appeal via the "counter-notice" procedure. Reports and appeals are accepted via the wish wall (一念之墙) or legal@dreamlight.com.hk.

9. Supplementary

These Guidelines, together with the Terms of Service and the Privacy Guidelines, form the complete understanding for your use of the Gallery. They may be revised from time to time (detailed lists of each chapter's prohibitions will be supplemented successively); revisions will be published on this page. Let us guard this clear gallery of B612 together.
