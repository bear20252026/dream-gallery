# Sheep companion

The user supplied `Downloads/sheep (1).glb`; the download remains unchanged. `sheep-companion.glb` retains the original meshes, materials and attribution metadata, with meshopt compression and position/normal quantization for delivery (787,976 → 158,264 bytes). No mesh simplification is performed. Rebuild with `node scripts/optimize/build-sheep-assets.cjs [source.glb]`.

The game adds normalization, four leg pivots, head motion and companion behavior at runtime. In memories the sheep is an imagined companion, visible only to the player; the real sheep stays inside the box. Model attribution is also shown in the interaction button's title and journey help.
