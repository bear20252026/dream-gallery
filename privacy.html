Preamble

Our mission is to keep every treasured memory gently stored. B612 — a gallery for unfinished drawings (the "Gallery") is published by China (Shanghai) Pilot Free Trade Zone Dreamlight Digital Culture & Technology Interactive Entertainment International United Holding Group Co., Ltd. and operated together with its global teams. We take personal information protection seriously and keep improving it.

Please read and understand these Guidelines before using the Gallery. You may choose not to use the Gallery; continued use is deemed acceptance of these Guidelines.

The Gallery follows the "minimum necessary" principle. Key points:

  • We explain, per feature, what information is involved, why, and how;
  • Core experiences such as the quiz, entry gate and opening film run in your browser or are used only for the single purpose you triggered;
  • Some content you upload is stored on the platform solely for display, with access controlled by the Gallery's display rules;
  • You may correct your nickname, request deletion of uploaded content, clear local data, or stop using the Gallery at any time.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

The following are not necessary for the service and we undertake never to collect them:

1. Precise location. No GPS, base-station or Wi-Fi list location data.

2. Biometrics. No face, voice-print, fingerprint or iris recognition is implemented or enabled, and no such biometric features are collected.

3. Device-unique identifiers. We do not read IMEI, IMSI, device serial numbers, MAC addresses, Android ID, OAID or IDFA; we do not read contacts, call logs, SMS, calendars, photo albums or app lists.

4. Behavioural profiling. We do not profile your interests and do not provide personalised advertising or recommendations.

2. Information We Collect & Store (Server-Side)

The following are stored on the platform's servers, each for the stated purpose only:

1. Uploaded content. Photos, videos and links you upload are stored so they can be displayed on the gallery walls. Access is controlled by the Gallery's display rules (demonstration content is public; your own uploads are access-controlled). Each item is recorded with its uploader and timestamp. Deletion follows the "request + platform handling" mechanism described in the Terms of Service.

2. Chat and wish-wall messages. Messages you send in the chat room (only the most recent 100 per room are kept) and wishes you post on the wish wall are stored and publicly visible, as these features are public by nature.

3. Quiz submissions. Your quiz session, chosen answers and score are submitted for grading (rule-based or AI-assisted) and for your sky progress. They are used for grading and progress only — not for profiling.

4. Device identity (minimal). To remember you between visits and merge identities when you switch browsers, we compute a device key from your browser User-Agent and store a random cookie (vid) in your browser; on entry you may also submit a client-side display/timezone/canvas hash used only for identity merging. No IMEI/IMSI/GPS-type identifiers are involved.

5. Security & abuse-prevention logs. Time, IP address and User-Agent of requests are recorded to prevent abuse (rate limits, upload attack prevention) and for legal compliance. They are not used for behavioural profiling.

6. Client error reports. When a script error occurs, the error message is sent to the platform to fix bugs; it may contain page state but is not used to identify you.

7. Quota counters. Per-device daily counters for AI captioning (20/day) and voice synthesis (30/day) to prevent abuse.

3. Information Processed Locally Only

The following are processed and stored only in your browser's local storage and never synced to the server:

  • Your sky progress, star-mote and spirit-mote collection status;
  • Gallery preferences such as house coloring and view settings;
  • Your reading/consent marks for the Terms of Service and Privacy Guidelines;
  • Your nickname, until you choose to display it publicly in features you trigger.

Clearing browser data removes the above permanently; it does not affect the basic availability of the Gallery.

4. AI Services

AI (third-party large-model services, including but not limited to the Moonshot Kimi family) is used for: quiz grading; automatic captioning of uploaded photos; and chat AI replies. Uploaded photos may be transmitted to the AI service for caption generation; they are used only for that generation, not for training other purposes by us. AI output is for reference only. You may refuse AI features; refusal does not affect other functions.

5. Third-Party Basic Services

For page quality, media speed and voice features, the Gallery uses:

  • Cloudflare CDN & R2 storage: caching and nearby delivery of media files; not used to profile you;
  • Text-to-speech service: converts fixed page texts to speech; carries no personal information about you;
  • AI large-model services: as described in section 4.

Otherwise we do not share or transfer your information to third parties, and no ad networks, analytics services or third-party social logins are integrated.

Under applicable laws and national standards, we may process relevant information without prior consent where: necessary for concluding/performing a contract with you; necessary for statutory duties or obligations; necessary for emergency response to protect life, health or property; processing information you have made public or that is lawfully public, within a reasonable scope; or other circumstances provided by law.

6. Storage

1. Scope & purpose. Server-side storage is limited to the items in section 2, each stored only for the stated display/security purpose. No personal-information dossier is otherwise built or maintained.

2. Retention. Information is kept no longer than necessary for the stated purposes; upon your deletion request or withdrawal of consent we delete it within a reasonable period. Where laws require longer retention (compliance, audit, disputes), we keep it for that mandated period, then delete or anonymise it.

3. Region. Information collected and generated within mainland China is stored within mainland China; no cross-border transfer is currently involved. If ever needed, we will separately inform you (purpose, recipient, method, safeguards, risks) and obtain your consent.

4. Shutdown. If the Gallery ceases operation, we will stop collection and delete or anonymise held information within a reasonable period; anonymised data is no longer personal information.

5. Security. We take all reasonable and feasible measures to protect information, including access control, least privilege and tiered credential management; please understand no measure guarantees absolute security.

7. Cookies & Similar Technologies

The Gallery uses cookies and browser local storage (localStorage/sessionStorage) on your device to: remember your session state and nickname; save your preferences and progress; and record your reading/consent status for the Terms of Service and Privacy Guidelines.

These technologies are strictly necessary for the above functions and carry no tracking or advertising purpose. You may manage or clear cookies and site data in browser settings; some preferences and progress will be unrecoverable, but basic Gallery functions remain usable.

8. Your Rights

During your use of the Gallery you have the following rights:

  • Access & correction: change your nickname anytime via the compass → settings; progress and preferences are stored in your browser and viewable anytime;
  • Deletion: request deletion of your uploaded content via the wish wall or page contacts — handled within 15 working days after verification; local data can be cleared instantly via browser settings;
  • Withdrawal of consent: stop using the Gallery at any time (deemed withdrawal); clearing browser site data restores the first-reading flow of the Terms and Guidelines;
  • Complaints & suggestions: submit via the contacts below; we verify necessary information and answer within 15 working days.

13. Glossary

Personal information: various information recorded electronically or otherwise relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, excluding anonymised information.

Sensitive personal information: information whose leakage or unlawful use could easily harm a person's dignity or personal/property safety, including biometrics, religious beliefs, specific identity, medical health, financial accounts, location tracks, and personal information of minors under 14.

Device information: usually includes device brand/model, OS type and version, language, resolution, network access type, IP address, browser type and settings, UA, etc. (the Gallery only uses the browser UA from these).

SSL: a security protocol above transport communication protocols achieving confidential transmission via public-key and symmetric-key technologies.

Cookies & similar technologies: small files containing strings and other technologies achieving similar purposes, stored on your device to remember your state and preferences.

Anonymisation: technical processing of personal information such that the subject cannot be identified or associated and the result cannot be restored.

De-identification: technical processing of personal information such that it cannot identify or be associated with a subject without additional information.

十三、名词解释

个人信息:以电子或者其他方式记录的与已识别或者可识别的自然人有关的各种信息,不包括匿名化处理后的信息。

敏感个人信息:一旦泄露或者非法使用,容易导致自然人的人格尊严受到侵害或者人身、财产安全受到危害的个人信息,包括生物识别、宗教信仰、特定身份、医疗健康、金融账户、行踪轨迹等信息,以及不满十四周岁未成年人的个人信息。

设备信息:通常包括设备品牌、设备型号、操作系统类型及版本、语言设置、分辨率、网络接入方式、IP 地址、浏览器类型和设置、UA 等(本画廊仅使用其中的浏览器 UA 信息)。

SSL:在传输通信协议之上实现的一种安全协议,通过公开密钥与对称密钥技术实现信息保密传输。

Cookie 及同类技术:包含字符串的小文件及可实现类似用途的其他技术,存放于您的设备内,用于记住您的状态与偏好。

匿名化:通过对个人信息的技术处理,使得个人信息主体无法被识别或者关联,且处理后的信息不能被复原的过程。

去标识化:通过对个人信息的技术处理,使其在不借助额外信息的情况下,无法识别或关联个人信息主体的过程。
